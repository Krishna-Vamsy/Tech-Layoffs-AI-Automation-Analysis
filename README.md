Tech Layoffs & AI Automation Analysis (2020–2026)

This project analyzes a dataset of 1,850 global tech layoff events between 2020 and 2026 to track the growing impact of AI automation on workforce reductions and measure the short-term stock market reaction to these announcements.

📌 Project Overview

AI Shift: AI-driven restructuring expanded from representing 11% of total industry job cuts to 38%.

Market Sentiment: Public companies announcing AI-related layoffs experienced a median 1-week stock price change of -2.5%, compared to -1.9% for traditional restructurings.

Data Processing: Cleaned and standardized data across public and private firms, consolidated 49 overlapping sector categories, and segmented companies by size tiers.

Visualizations: Created custom Matplotlib and Seaborn charts to highlight timeline spikes, departmental impacts, and corporate restructuring trends.

🛠 Tools Used

Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

Environment: Jupyter Notebook
